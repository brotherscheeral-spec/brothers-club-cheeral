⚽ Our Story

Brotherhood Born on
the Football Field

Brothers Club Cheeral (Reg. No: 27/88) was founded in 1988 in Cheeral, Kerala — born from a shared love of football and a spirit of brotherhood that has never faded. What began as a group of passionate young men kicking a ball on open grounds has grown into a proud institution with decades of memories, trophies, and lasting friendships.

Over more than 37 years, the club has nurtured local talent, competed in regional tournaments, and built a legacy that lives on through every generation of players. Brothers Club is more than a football team — it's a family.
